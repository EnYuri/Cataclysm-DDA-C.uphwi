# Community Rules (Local)

- Informal or formal language is both welcome—just don’t be a jerk.
- NSFW content is allowed. Keep it consensual, clearly labeled, and away from minors.
- Politics stays out. If it turns political, it gets shut down.
- No harassment, targeted hate, doxxing, or stalking.
- No spam, baiting, or endless drama. Take personal disputes to DMs.
- Moderator/maintainer decisions are final.
